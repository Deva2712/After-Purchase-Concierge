Thank you for choosing the SwiftBoil K1 cordless kettle. Read this manual before first use. The troubleshooting section on page 3 covers the lid, heating and switching problems that account for almost all support requests, and most are fixed in a minute.

Important safety instructions

  • Use the kettle only with the supplied power base. Do not use the base with any other appliance.
  • Fill between the MIN (0.5 L) and MAX (1.7 L) marks. Overfilling can cause boiling water to spit from the spout. Switching on empty triggers the boil-dry protection.
  • Keep the lid closed while boiling. Steam from the spout and lid area is hot; keep hands and face away.
  • Do not immerse the kettle or base in water. Keep the connector in the base dry.
  • Place on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from the edge of the counter and out of reach of children.
  • Unplug the base when not in use and before cleaning.

Parts

The lid is held closed by a spring latch at the front. A silicone seal sits in a channel around the underside of the lid and presses against the rim of the kettle. The on/off switch is under the handle; a small lever behind it is the lid interlock, which stops the switch latching when the lid is open. The power light inside the switch glows while heating.

Before first use

  1. Remove all packaging, including the card insert inside the lid.
  2. Fill to MAX, boil, and discard the water. Repeat once. This rinses the heating plate.

Everyday use

  1. Press the lid button to open the lid. Fill between MIN and MAX. Press the lid down at the centre until the latch clicks.
  2. Place the kettle on the base. It seats in any direction.
  3. Push the switch fully down until it clicks and the light comes on. The kettle switches off automatically when the water boils.
  4. Lift the kettle off the base to pour. Pour slowly; the scale filter slows the flow slightly.
Boil-dry protection: if the kettle is switched on with too little water, a thermostat cuts the power and locks the switch out. Fill the kettle, let it cool for 10 minutes, and it will work again. See page 3.

Descaling

Scale builds up on the heating plate and the lid rim, especially in hard-water areas. Scale on the plate makes the kettle switch off before boiling or not heat at all; scale on the rim stops the lid sealing. Descale monthly, or when you can see white deposits.

  1. Fill to MIN with equal parts water and white vinegar, or use a kettle descaler as directed.
  2. Boil, then leave to stand for 30 minutes. Pour away.
  3. Rinse by boiling two full kettles of fresh water and discarding them.
  4. Wipe the lid rim and the lid seal with a damp cloth to remove softened scale.

Care and cleaning

  • Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners on the stainless steel.
  • Pull the scale filter up and out of the spout to rinse it under the tap. Push it back down until it clicks.
  • Check the lid seal every few months. If it is hard, cracked, swollen or has come out of its channel, replace it with part SBK1-LIDSEAL. The seal pulls out by hand and the new one presses in; no tools are needed.

Troubleshooting

Work through the steps in order. Let the kettle cool before handling the lid.

The lid will not close or pops open (lid does not latch)

  1. Clean the rim. Wipe the kettle rim and the underside of the lid with a damp cloth to remove scale and debris. A thin layer of scale is enough to hold the lid a few millimetres open so the latch cannot catch.
  2. Check the lid seal. Look at the silicone ring in the channel under the lid. Press it back into the channel all the way round so it sits evenly and does not bulge anywhere.
  3. Replace the seal if it is swollen, hardened, torn or will not stay in its channel. Pull the old seal out by hand and press the new lid seal (part SBK1-LIDSEAL) into the channel, starting opposite the hinge. This is the usual fix for a lid that used to close and now pops open.
  4. Close the lid correctly. Press down at the centre of the lid, not the front edge, until you hear the latch click, and make sure the lid button has fully sprung back up.

If the lid still will not latch with a new seal fitted, check that both hinge pins are in their slots at the back of the lid. If a pin is bent or missing, contact the retailer.

The kettle will not heat (water stays cold, light off or switches off at once)

  1. Check the base. Make sure the base is plugged into a working outlet and the kettle is sitting flat on it. Lift the kettle off and put it back; the light should come on when you press the switch.
  2. Reset the boil-dry protection. If the kettle was switched on empty or nearly empty, the thermostat has cut out. Fill to at least MIN, wait 10 minutes for the plate to cool, then switch on again.
  3. Check the lid and switch. The switch will not latch while the lid is open. Close the lid until it clicks, then push the switch fully down until it clicks and the light comes on.
  4. Descale. Heavy scale on the heating plate makes the thermostat trip early, so the kettle clicks off after a few seconds or never gets hot. Follow the descaling procedure on page 2, then try again.

If the light comes on but the water never warms, or the light never comes on from a working outlet with the lid closed, the heating element or thermostat has failed. Contact the retailer; this is covered by the warranty on page 4.

The kettle switches off before the water boils

  1. Descale as on page 2. This is the cause in almost every case.
  2. Make sure the lid is fully closed; steam escaping from an open lid never reaches the switch-off sensor.
  3. Fill to at least MIN. Very small amounts of water boil off before the sensor reacts.

Water tastes or smells odd

  1. Boil and discard two full kettles of fresh water.
  2. Descale, then rinse again.
  3. Remove and rinse the scale filter.

Limited warranty

Warranty period: 24 months from the date of purchase shown on your order.

During the warranty period the merchant will repair or replace, at its choice and at no charge, a kettle that fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship under normal household use. This includes the heating element, thermostat, switch, lid latch and hinge, and the power base.

What the warranty does not cover

  • The lid seal and scale filter, which are consumable parts sold separately.
  • Faults caused by scale build-up where the kettle has not been descaled as described on page 2.
  • Damage from drops, immersion in water, or use with a base other than the one supplied.

How to make a warranty claim

  1. Try the troubleshooting steps on page 3, including descaling.
  2. Contact the retailer where you bought the kettle with your order number and a short description of the fault.
  3. The merchant replies within 2 business days with a repair or replacement decision and a prepaid shipping label when needed.

Returns

Separate from this warranty, products can be returned to the retailer for a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days, faults are handled under this warranty.
