Important safety instructions

  • Listen at moderate volume. Long exposure to loud sound can damage hearing.
  • Do not use noise cancelling where you need to hear your surroundings, such as when cycling or crossing roads.
  • Charge only with the supplied USB-C cable from a 5 V source. Do not charge while the headphones are wet.
  • The battery is built in. Do not open the earcups; there are no user-serviceable parts inside.
  • Keep away from heat above 45 °C, for example a car dashboard in the sun.

Charging

Charge fully before first use (about 2 hours). The light is red while charging and turns off when full. A 10-minute charge gives about 3 hours of playback. Pairing and noise cancelling are disabled when the battery is below 5%.

Pairing for the first time

  1. With the headphones off, press and hold the Power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue and white alternately. The headphones say "pairing".
  2. On your phone or computer, open the Bluetooth settings and choose QuietWave H2 from the list.
  3. The light turns steady blue and the headphones say "connected". The H2 remembers up to 2 devices and reconnects to the last one automatically when switched on.

Wearing and the fold sensor

Rotate both earcups flat to fold the headphones into the case. A sensor in the right earcup hinge pauses playback and, after 2 minutes, switches the headphones off when they are folded flat. Make sure both earcups are rotated fully into the listening position before use; a half-rotated right cup can leave that side muted.

Wired listening

Plug the audio cable firmly into the socket on the left earcup until it clicks, and into your device. Wired listening works with the battery flat. Noise cancelling needs battery power in either mode.

Care

  • Wipe the earcups and headband with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use solvents.
  • Clean the driver mesh inside each earcup with a dry brush if sound becomes quiet or muffled on one side.
  • The ear cushions clip off for cleaning: pull gently from the inner edge. Press back on until they click all the way round.
  • Store in the case. Charge at least every 3 months when not in use.

Troubleshooting

Work through the steps in order and test after each one.

No sound on one side (one earcup is silent)

  1. Check the audio balance on your device. On a phone this is under Accessibility, Audio, Balance; on a computer it is in the sound output settings. Set the slider to the centre.
  2. If you are using the audio cable, unplug both ends and push them back in firmly until they click. Then test wirelessly: if both sides work wirelessly, the cable is faulty and should be replaced.
  3. Check that the right earcup is rotated fully into the listening position. The fold sensor in the right hinge mutes that side when the cup is turned flat or left half-way.
  4. Reset the headphones. With them switched on, hold the Power button and Volume Down together for 10 seconds until the light flashes purple. Then pair again as on page 2. A reset clears a stuck audio channel.

If one side is still silent after a reset in both wired and wireless modes, the driver has failed. Contact the retailer; this is covered by the warranty on page 4.

The headphones will not pair or connect

  1. On your device, open the Bluetooth list, find QuietWave H2, and choose "Forget" or "Remove". Old pairing records are the most common cause of connection failures.
  2. Put the headphones into pairing mode: switch them off, then hold the Power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue and white alternately. Pairing mode is different from simply switching on.
  3. Clear the pairing list on the headphones if they still do not appear: hold Power and Volume Up together for 8 seconds until the light flashes white three times. Then repeat step 2.
  4. Charge for at least 15 minutes. Pairing is disabled below 5% battery, and the light does not come on at all when the battery is empty.

Keep the device within 1 metre during pairing, and switch off Bluetooth on other nearby devices that were previously paired. If the headphones never show the alternating blue and white light, contact the retailer.

The battery runs down quickly

  1. Switch off noise cancelling when you do not need it; it uses about a third of the battery.
  2. Fold the headphones flat when not in use so the fold sensor switches them off.
  3. Charge fully, then time a full discharge at moderate volume. Under 20 hours with noise cancelling off indicates a battery fault covered by the warranty.

Hiss or pressure feeling with noise cancelling on

  1. Check the ear cushions are clipped on all the way round. A gap lets wind noise reach the microphones.
  2. Press the ANC button to switch to transparency or off in very quiet rooms, where the low-frequency hiss is most noticeable.

Limited warranty

Warranty period: 12 months from the date of purchase shown on your order.

During the warranty period the merchant will repair or replace, at its choice and at no charge, headphones that fail because of a defect in materials or workmanship under normal use. This includes the drivers, the battery (if capacity falls below 60% of the rated figure), the Bluetooth module and the controls.

What the warranty does not cover

  • Ear cushions and cables, which are consumable parts sold separately.
  • Damage from liquid, drops, heat, or opening the earcups.
  • Normal cosmetic wear of the headband and cushions.

How to make a warranty claim

  1. Try the troubleshooting steps on page 3, including a reset.
  2. Contact the retailer where you bought the headphones with your order number and a short description of the fault.
  3. The merchant replies within 2 business days with a repair or replacement decision and a prepaid shipping label when needed.

Returns

Separate from this warranty, products can be returned to the retailer for a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days, faults are handled under this warranty.
