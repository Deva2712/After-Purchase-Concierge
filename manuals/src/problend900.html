Important safety instructions

  • Use only on a dry, level surface. Keep the motor base away from water; never immerse it.
  • Always lock the jar onto the base and press the lid fully closed before switching on. The blender will not start unless both safety switches are engaged.
  • Never put hands or utensils into the jar while the blender is plugged in. The blades are sharp; handle the blade assembly by its plastic collar.
  • Do not blend hot liquids above 60 °C with the lid cap fitted; steam pressure can lift the lid. Fill to no more than half and start on the lowest speed.
  • Do not run the motor continuously for more than 60 seconds. Allow 1 minute of rest between cycles.
  • Unplug after use, before cleaning, and before fitting or removing parts.

Setting up

  1. Wash the jar, blade assembly, lid and cap in warm soapy water. Dry all parts. Wipe the motor base with a damp cloth only.
  2. Fit the blade assembly into the bottom of the jar. Turn the black collar clockwise until it is snug. A loose collar is the most common cause of leaks.
  3. Place the jar on the motor base so that the arrow on the jar base points at the unlock mark, then turn the jar clockwise until it clicks at the lock mark. The jar switch is now engaged.
  4. Fit the lid and press down firmly on both sides until the tab sits in the handle slot. The lid switch is now engaged and the power light turns steady white.
  5. Plug the blender into a grounded outlet. The power light flashes once and then stays on.

Everyday use

  • Add liquids first, then soft ingredients, then ice or frozen items on top. Never fill above the MAX line (1.5 L).
  • Turn the dial to speed 1 to start, then increase. Use Pulse for short bursts and for crushing ice.
  • To add ingredients while blending, remove the measuring cap and pour through the lid opening. Keep the lid itself in place.
  • Thick mixtures: stop the motor, use the tamper through the lid opening, then restart.
Thermal protection: the motor switches itself off if it overheats, for example after a long run with a heavy load. See "The blender will not turn on" on page 3 for the reset procedure.

Care and cleaning

  • Quick clean: half fill the jar with warm water and a drop of detergent, run on speed 2 for 20 seconds, rinse.
  • Deep clean: remove the blade assembly by turning the collar anticlockwise. Remove the silicone seal from the collar, wash both, dry, and refit the seal flat in its groove before reassembling.
  • The jar, lid, cap and tamper are dishwasher safe on the top rack. The blade assembly and seal should be washed by hand so the seal keeps its shape.
  • Inspect the seal monthly. Replace it, or the complete blade assembly (part PB900-BLADE), if it is cracked, flattened or has lost its shape.

Troubleshooting

Work through the steps in order. Most problems are solved by step 1 or 2. Unplug the blender before touching the blade assembly.

The blender will not turn on (no power, nothing happens, stopped working)

  1. Check that the jar is locked. Lift the jar off, place it back with the arrow at the unlock mark, and turn it clockwise until it clicks at the lock mark. The blender cannot start unless the jar switch is pressed.
  2. Check that the lid is fully closed. Press down on both sides of the lid until the tab sits in the handle slot. The power light turns steady white when the lid switch is engaged.
  3. Check the power supply. Plug the blender into a different outlet that you know works. If the power light stays off on a working outlet, go to step 4.
  4. Reset the thermal protection. Unplug the blender and let it cool for 30 minutes. Plug it back in, then press and hold the Pulse button for 5 seconds until the power light flashes twice. The motor is now reset.

If the blender still does not turn on after these four steps, the motor or control board needs service. Contact the retailer; this is covered by the warranty on page 4.

The blender is leaking (liquid under the jar or on the motor base)

  1. Tighten the blade assembly. Empty the jar, turn it over, and turn the black collar clockwise until it is snug. Do not use tools.
  2. Check the seal. Remove the blade assembly and lift the silicone seal out of the collar. Clean away any food trapped underneath, and look for cracks or flat spots. Refit the seal so that it lies flat all the way round.
  3. If the seal is cracked, flattened or missing, replace the blade assembly with seal (part PB900-BLADE). This is a two-minute fix and does not need tools.
  4. Check the fill level. Do not fill above the MAX line, and fill hot liquids to no more than half. Overfilling forces liquid past the lid and down the outside of the jar, which looks like a leak from the base.

If liquid still appears under the jar with a new seal fitted, inspect the glass for a hairline crack near the base. A cracked jar must not be used; contact the retailer.

The motor runs but the blades do not spin

  1. Stop the motor and unplug the blender. Remove the jar and check that the drive coupling under the jar turns freely by hand.
  2. Look for a large piece of food or ice wedged under the blade. Remove it with the tamper, never with your fingers.
  3. Add more liquid; very thick mixtures stall the blades. Restart on speed 1 and use Pulse.

Burning smell or the motor sounds strained

  1. Switch off at once and unplug. Let the blender rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Reduce the load: blend in smaller batches and add liquid before solids.
  3. If the smell returns under a normal load, stop using the blender and contact the retailer.

Limited warranty

Warranty period: 12 months from the date of purchase shown on your order.

During the warranty period the merchant will repair or replace, at its choice and at no charge, any part that fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship under normal household use. This includes the motor, the control board, the interlock switches and the jar.

What the warranty does not cover

  • Normal wear of the silicone seal and blade edge. These are consumable parts and are sold separately.
  • Damage from misuse, overfilling, blending hot liquids above 60 °C, dropping, or using the blender for anything other than food preparation.
  • Products that have been opened or repaired by anyone other than the merchant.

How to make a warranty claim

  1. Try the troubleshooting steps on page 3 first; they solve most faults.
  2. Contact the retailer where you bought the blender with your order number and a short description of the fault. No receipt is needed if the order is on file.
  3. The merchant replies within 2 business days with a repair or replacement decision and, when needed, a prepaid shipping label.

Returns

Separate from this warranty, unused or faulty products can be returned to the retailer for a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days, faults are handled under this warranty.
